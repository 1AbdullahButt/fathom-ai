Key rules: one teal accent; AI text in Instrument Sans ink, human-edited text in Newsreader pencil-blue; every AI claim shows its QuoteChip; missing owner/date = "not stated"; low confidence = amber flag; timestamps in IBM Plex Mono; no emoji, no gradients, no bots.
