# Submission checklist (from the 8x brief)

Deadline: 25 Oct 2026, 07:16 UTC (12:16 PKT). Clock is tracked, not enforced.

## Before writing code
- [ ] Use Fathom properly, with screenshots into research/:
      calendar connected, notetaker in a real 2-min call with yourself, playback vs transcript,
      summary + switch templates, action items, highlight mid-call, search across meetings,
      share a clip with someone not on the call.
- [ ] Long-call case: start a Meet with yourself, share a tab playing a ~1-hour panel/meeting

      Note what breaks or gets hard to use; that becomes your decisions.
- [ ] 8x agent capture setup done, capture test passed, .agent-logs/ committed.

## Seed data (an empty list fails)

      to reuse, e.g. a public-record government meeting or an openly licensed meeting corpus.
      Check the licence and say where it came from in the README.
